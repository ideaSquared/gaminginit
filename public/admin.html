How flags work
  1. The bot watches by itself. Each player’s Steam account is checked with Steam as they join, and every kill in the kill feed is checked for headshots, team kills and vehicle suicides.
  2. Serious ones go to Discord. Players passing a griefing mark and the riskiest Steam accounts are posted in the moderation log channel as they happen.
  3. Everything is listed here, those in game first; the rest are folded under Not in game. A flag is a reason to look, not proof: check the player’s history before you act.

        Link your Steam account

        Seeder VIP and the top seeders are for the players who seed, so staff are not counted. Link the Steam account you play on, and the bot counts your time on the server as playing, not seeding. You won’t earn seeder VIP or show as a top seeder. Your kills, matches and time played show as before. You get a staff spot instead: a reserved slot on the server for as long as you’re linked.

        17 digits, starting 7656119. Or paste your Steam profile’s link, if it has that number in it.

        How to find it
        1. In the Steam app, click your name at the top right, then Account details. Your Steam ID is the 17-digit number under your account name. Copy it and paste it above.
        2. Or open your Steam profile, right-click anywhere on it and pick Copy Page URL. If the link has /profiles/ and a number in it, paste the whole link above.
        3. If your link is steamcommunity.com/id/ and a name, you set a custom URL, so the number is not in it. Use Account details, or paste your link into steamid.io and copy the steamID64.
