              Good to know

              • Coins go to your Steam account as you earn them, linked or not. Link it on your account to spend them.
              • Whitelist is added to the days you have left. You can hold up to 14 days at once, so it can’t be stockpiled.
              • The server only reads the whitelist when it restarts, so it starts at the next restart, usually the daily one.
              • Staff have a whitelist spot already, so they don’t earn or spend coins. Private profiles are left off the board.
              • You’ll get a message in game when you finish a mission, and in the Discord with /coins, /shop and /buy.
