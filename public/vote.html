Vote in Discord

A vote opens in our Discord when a match goes live and stays open until a team is close to winning (95 points). Press a map's button in the vote channel to vote, and the time of day and weather buttons under it (each has a Random); press another to change any of them. Where no lighting has both the time and the weather chosen, the weather wins. The circles are not voted on: the winning map gets a random layout, never the one it had last time. You can do the same here: sign in with Discord and press a map. You vote with your Discord account, so it is one vote each, wherever you press it, and only members of the Discord can vote. The map with the most votes is put on the server and plays when this match ends. This page shows the same counts.
