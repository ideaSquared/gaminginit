Rules of engagement

Rules

Play hard, play fair. Break these and an admin will be along to have a word.

  1. 01

    No cheats

    No aimbots, wallhacks or any other hacks.

  2. 02

    No exploits

    Don’t use bugs or glitches to get an edge.

  3. 03

    No team kills

    Don’t kill your own team on purpose.

  4. 04

    No dropping crates in HQ

    Keep crates out of the HQ.

  5. 05

    No griefing

    Don’t spoil the match for others, such as by blocking, trolling or wrecking your own team’s vehicles.

  6. 06

    No racism

    No racism or hate speech, in chat or in your name.

  7. 07

    No abuse

    Be decent to other players and to admins.

Admins have the final say

If an admin tells you to do something, do it. Breaking the rules can get you warned, kicked or banned. Think a call was wrong? Ask in our Discord.
