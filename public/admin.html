How alerts work

  1. The bot watches by itself. Each player’s Steam account is checked with Steam as they join, and every death in the kill feed is checked for team kills and vehicle suicides.
  2. Serious ones go to Discord. Players passing a griefing mark are posted in the moderation log channel as they happen, with their Steam account’s risk. Of the Steam accounts, only the riskiest are posted by themselves.
  3. Everything is listed here, riskiest first. An alert is a reason to look, not proof: check the player before you act.
